BAIT: Large Language Model Backdoor Scanning by Inverting Attack Target

October 19, 2025

The paper “BAIT: Large Language Model Backdoor Scanning by Inverting Attack Target” addresses backdoor (trojan) attacks in LLMs by proactively detecting hidden triggers through attack target inversion.

Core Innovation#

  • Backdoor detection via inversion: Instead of waiting for malicious prompts, BAIT reconstructs potential trojan triggers to expose hidden vulnerabilities.
  • Non-intrusive scanning: Works without retraining, allowing safe inspection of deployed models.

Architectural Highlights#

  • Scans models across layers to detect vulnerabilities.
  • Works efficiently on models up to 13B parameters, demonstrating scalability to large LLMs.

Comparison & Key Insights#

  • Detection accuracy: BAIT achieves ~90%, outperforming prior methods:
    • STRIP: ~70%
    • Standard anomaly detection: ~65%
  • Backdoors can exist even in models that appear safe.
  • Inverting the attack target is a more systematic and reliable approach than previous defenses.
