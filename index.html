Large Concept Models (LCMs): A Paradigm Shift Beyond Token-Level Language Modeling

Standard LLMs operate at the token level—chunking text into words or subwords and predicting the next token. But human thought works differently: we think in concepts, flows, and structures, not just discrete units. Core Innovation Semantic-level reasoning: The Large Concept Model (LCM) handles text at the sentence (concept) level instead of tokens, using the SONAR multilingual and multimodal embedding space as its foundation. Language and modality-agnostic abstraction: Concepts are universal—independent of language and format—enabling the same reasoning pipeline for text or speech across 200 languages.
