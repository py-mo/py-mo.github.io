It all started in 2019, just before the world went into lockdown. While friends were playing games or scrolling endlessly through social media, I stumbled upon a documentary about **Artificial Intelligence**. That one hour changed everything. Suddenly, I realized that AI wasn’t just a buzzword — it was a doorway into a world where **mathematics, logic, and creativity** collide.

## My Journey

From that moment, curiosity turned into passion. I started exploring programming with **Python, C, and C++**, later experimenting with **Rust**. Each language taught me something new, but what truly fascinated me was the **why behind AI** — the mathematics, the algorithms, and the reasoning that makes machines intelligent.  

By 2022, AI had become my clear focus. I immersed myself in:  

- **Machine Learning & Mathematical Foundations** — understanding the principles behind algorithms, probabilities, and models  
- **Explainable AI (XAI)** — making AI systems transparent, interpretable, and trustworthy  
- **Agents & Large Language Models (LLMs)** — exploring new ways of interacting with knowledge and data  

Through this journey, I’ve learned that mastering AI is not just about coding — it’s about thinking critically, experimenting relentlessly, and never being afraid to question how things work.

## Beyond Code

While AI dominates my professional focus, my inspiration comes from many places. **Formula 1 racing**, for example, teaches lessons in precision, innovation, and relentless improvement. **Reading and writing** allow me to reflect on my learning and share ideas with clarity.  

I believe that **interdisciplinary thinking** — connecting patterns from racing, literature, and mathematics — is what makes problem-solving in AI both powerful and creative.

## What’s Next

This website is where I plan to share my journey, insights, and experiments. Expect **project walkthroughs**, **deep dives into machine learning mathematics**, explorations of **LLMs and AI agents**, and reflections on how ideas from other fields can inspire innovation in technology.

If you’re curious about AI, math, or the stories behind how machines “think,” you’re in the right place. Join me on this journey — it’s going to be both challenging and exciting.
