I began programming in 2019, just before the COVID-19 pandemic. When lockdowns started in 2020, while many turned to games and entertainment, I found myself drawn to a documentary about Artificial Intelligence — and that moment changed everything. What started as simple curiosity quickly became a lifelong passion.  

Since then, I have explored multiple programming languages, including **Python, C, C++, and Rust**, building a strong foundation in software development. However, what truly excites me is the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics** — understanding not just how algorithms work, but *why* they work.  

By 2022, I had made AI my central focus. My journey has led me to become familiar with several key areas:  

- **Machine Learning & Mathematical Foundations**: Delving into algorithms, statistics, and the mathematical principles that underpin intelligent systems.  
- **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Building transparent, interpretable, and trustworthy models that can be understood and verified.  
- **Agents & Large Language Models (LLMs)**: Exploring cutting-edge approaches to interaction, reasoning, and knowledge retrieval.  
- **AI System Design & Deployment**: Designing and implementing practical AI solutions, from research prototypes to production-ready applications.  

I approach every project with a mindset of **deep learning and experimentation**, focusing on both theoretical understanding and real-world impact.  

Outside of computer science, I find inspiration in **Formula 1 racing**, a sport that embodies precision, innovation, and relentless pursuit of improvement. I also enjoy **reading and writing**, which allow me to reflect, consolidate knowledge, and communicate ideas with clarity and depth.  

My journey is ongoing — I am constantly seeking new challenges, learning opportunities, and ways to contribute to the growing world of AI and technology.
